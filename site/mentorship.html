Who this is for

If you have already achieved it, and it did not do what it promised.

Trish works with iconic women: the ones who chased the world’s definition of success, caught it, and paid for it in battles nobody watched. You do not need fixing. You need renewing, which is a different job and a harder one.

  • You are the strong one in every room and privately exhausted by it.
  • You have a message you have never said out loud at full volume.
  • You survived something you turned into competence instead of purpose.
  • You are entering a decade everyone treats as a decline, and you refuse that.

Questions

Before you apply

Is this coaching or mentorship?

Mentorship. Coaching asks you questions until you find your own answer. Trish has walked the road you are on, seven decades of it, and will tell you what she knows, then hold you to it.

What does it cost?

Investment is discussed on the fifteen minute call, once it is clear which room fits. Trish would rather quote you honestly than publish a number that answers the wrong question.

Do I have to be in Los Angeles?

No. Mentorship runs remotely by default, with in person sessions available in the Los Angeles area by arrangement.

Is this faith based?

Trish is openly a woman of faith. Divinely connected is not decoration. She mentors women of every background and will meet you where your own convictions are.

How is this connected to Safe Passage Heals?

Safe Passage Heals is the non-profit Trish founded and still leads, serving women and children escaping domestic violence. The mentorship practice is separate work, built on the same method.
