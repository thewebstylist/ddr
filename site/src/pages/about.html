The first act

Twenty five years making other people look like themselves

Trish Steele spent more than two decades inside the entertainment industry as a model, makeup artist and wardrobe stylist. A Hollywood beauty consultant whose job was to find the truest version of a person and put it on camera.

It turned out to be an apprenticeship for everything that came after. She learned that presence is not decoration. It is what happens when the inside and the outside finally agree.

The turn

Then she needed a safe passage of her own

Trish is a survivor of domestic violence. What she built afterwards did not begin as an organisation. It began as a small ministry run out of her own house, for women who had nowhere else to be that night.

It became Women Crowned In Glory, Inc., doing business as Safe Passage Heals. Since then, more than two thousand women and children have come through its doors looking for counselling and shelter, and for the thing that is harder to provide than either: a way to break the cycle rather than survive one more round of it.
