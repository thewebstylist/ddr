# Where this copy came from

**Read this before the site goes live.** The words on these pages are
reconstructed, not migrated, and some of them are proposals rather than facts.

## Why

`trishsteele.com` is blocked by the network egress policy of the environment
this was built in. Both `curl` and the fetch tool were refused at the proxy:

```
kind:   connect_rejected
detail: gateway answered 403 to CONNECT (policy denial)
host:   trishsteele.com:443
```

So the existing pages could not be crawled and the existing images could not be
downloaded. Everything below was assembled from search-indexed fragments of the
live site plus public profiles, then written up in Trish's voice.

## Deliberately avoided

- No testimonials. There were none to source, and inventing a quote and
  attributing it to a client would be fabrication.
- No press logos as images. The press strip is set in type, naming outlets that
  are publicly attested. Using their marks needs permission.
- No photographs of Trish were generated. Producing a synthetic likeness of a
  real person is not something to ship. Every slot waits for a real picture.
