What the mentorship rebuilds

Three renewals, in order

i.

Renew the mind

Name the self sabotage precisely, then dismantle it. The work is on the beliefs running underneath the achievements: the ones that made the summit feel like nothing when you got there.

ii.

Reclaim the woman

Twenty five years dressing and styling Hollywood taught Trish that presence is not vanity. It is authority made visible. You will be seen the way you actually are.

iii.

Rebuild the legacy

Your message, sharpened until it lands. Your platform, built so it outlasts you. What you survived becomes the thing that carries somebody else.

The Mind of Steele Mentorship

Placement by conversation, not by tier

The Reset

One private intensive

A single, unhurried session for the woman who knows something has to change and cannot yet name it. You leave with the belief that is in the way identified out loud, and the first honest thing to do about it.

  • One extended private session
  • A written renewal brief afterwards
  • One follow up inside thirty days

The Invincible Year

Twelve months, one to one

The whole method, at the pace real change actually takes. Twelve months through all three renewals, with Trish accountable to your outcome and you accountable to hers.

  • Private sessions twice monthly
  • Direct access between sessions
  • Presence and platform work
  • Your message built and taken somewhere real

The Crown Circle

Small group, by cohort

A deliberately small room of women at the same altitude, mentored together. Some things only ever get said in front of peers who have paid the same price.

  • Limited cohort, admission by application
  • Live group mentorship on a fixed cadence
  • One private session inside the term
