# BRIEF — TrishSteele.com

Interviewed (4 questions asked and answered), plus 4 earlier scoping answers.
Not self-authored.

## Interview answers

1. **Vibe** — "A couture house archive." Ivory paper, hairline rules, immense
   restraint punctuated by one saturated colour. Ageless, expensive, feminine
   without being soft. Chosen over cathedral / memoir / backstage-Vogue.
2. **Aesthetic distance** — Cinematic-immersive. Full-viewport acts, pinned
   sections, kinetic type assembling line by line.
3. **Structure** — Distinct chapters. Each act behaves differently; variety is
   the point. Explicitly NOT one unbroken world.
4. **Imagery** — REVISED BY USER MID-BUILD: no generated imagery at all. The
   user is assembling real photography of Trish (solo, at events, with others)
   over the next two days. Every image slot ships as a designed placeholder
   carrying the art direction, with a named shot brief, for a one-line swap.
5. **Primary CTA** — Mentorship as hero, speaking secondary. Lead action:
   "Apply for Mentorship". Low-friction secondary: "Book a 15-Minute Call".
6. **Content** — Reconstructed from search-indexed fragments; trishsteele.com is
   blocked by this environment's egress policy. See CONTENT.md for provenance.
7. **Codebase** — `site/` subdirectory of thewebstylist/ddr, branch
   claude/trish-steele-redesign-ka9fba.
8. **Assets on hand** — None yet; real photography arriving within two days.
   Foundation and style are the deliverable now.

## The tell-someone sentence

"It's the site where one red thread runs unbroken from the first screen to the
last, and everything you read is strung on it."

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | What on screen causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Arrival | Arrested, still | Full-bleed silk in raking light. Type enters slowly, one word per line. |
| 2 Recognition | Seen, uncomfortable | Frame holds dead still while the copy assembles line by line. |
| 3 The Turn | Weight, respect **(PEAK)** | Seven decades as a horizontal archive corridor travelled sideways. |
| 4 The Method | Clarity, order | Calm after the peak. Three panels wipe in on a held ground. |
| 5 The Rooms | Appraisal, choice | Cards stack and release under the thumb. |
| 6 Commitment | Resolve | The page stops moving and starts responding to the pointer. |

Act 2 is deliberately the quietest, immediately before the peak.

## The peak

Act 3. A visitor would say: "You scroll sideways down seventy years of her life
like walking an archive corridor, and the red thread follows you the whole way."
Largest scroll span on the page by a clear margin.

## Signature move — "The Thread"

A single oxblood thread, one continuous SVG path, drawn by scroll progress from
the first screen to the last. It begins as the ribbon lying in the hero
photograph, leaves the image and becomes the hairline rule under the first
heading, loops around the peak act as the corridor travels sideways, threads
between the three method panels, and terminates as the underline beneath the
final CTA. Bespoke to this page, not a parameter change to any kit device.

## Authored silence

The held beat at the end of Act 2, before the peak opens. Roughly 0.6vh of no
new information. Intentional, not dead scroll.
