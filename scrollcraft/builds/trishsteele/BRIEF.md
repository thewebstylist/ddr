# BRIEF — TrishSteele.com

Interviewed, then twice redirected by the client mid-build. This file records
where it actually landed, not where it started.

## Final direction

**Reference given by the client: marieforleo.com.** Bright, warm,
photography-forward and personality-led. Big serif headline with one italic
accent word, a single high-chroma CTA colour against neutral, pill buttons,
rounded cards, alternating cream / blush / white / dark section blocks, a press
strip, and a photo mosaic.

- **Palette** cream `#FCF8F1`, blush `#FBE9E4`, white, plum `#2B1526`.
  One accent: gold `#E8A02C` on every CTA. Rose `#C8446B` for highlights only.
- **Type** Bodoni Moda for headings and figures, Cormorant Garamond italic for
  the single accented word, Inter for reading. All self-hosted.
- **Shape** rounded, friendly. Pill buttons, 28px cards, soft shadows.
- **Primary CTA** Apply for Mentorship. Secondary: Book a 15 minute call.
  Speaking kept as its own destination rather than buried.
- **Imagery** none generated. The client is supplying real photography of Trish,
  solo and at events, within two days. Every slot ships reserved and captioned.

## Two rejected directions, and why

1. **Ivory and ink editorial.** Rejected as "the lazy Claude Code site styling":
   the ground was `#EDE7DC`, which is the default beige, and the composition was
   the house template (nav, hero, three cards, stat row, CTA band).
2. **Couture house archive.** Muted plaster, oxblood hairlines, scroll-drawn
   thread, pinned and lateral acts. Better built, still wrong: rejected as
   "drab". Quiet and archival is the opposite of the brief, which is
   *inspiring, strong, elegant, energetic*.

The lesson both times: the client wants **energy and warmth**, not restraint.

## Signature move — "The Gold Draw"

Every highlighted word on the site is underscored by a gold stroke that draws
itself, once, as the heading arrives. A single hand-drawn SVG path rather than a
border, so it has the wobble of something written instead of ruled. Nothing else
on the site uses the gesture.
